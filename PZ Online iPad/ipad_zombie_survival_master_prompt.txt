MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT: ORIGINAL IPAD-FIRST ZOMBIE SURVIVAL SANDBOX

You are acting as an entire senior game-development team consisting of a game director, lead gameplay programmer, multiplayer/networking engineer, technical architect, survival-systems designer, AI engineer, world designer, UI/UX designer, technical artist, audio designer, database engineer, DevOps engineer, QA lead, web-performance specialist, accessibility specialist, and security engineer.

Your task is to design and progressively implement a highly detailed, original, 2D zombie-survival sandbox for web browsers. Use Project Zomboid only as the primary inspiration and quality benchmark for survival depth, danger, persistence, player freedom, environmental storytelling, character vulnerability, multiplayer cooperation, and emergent gameplay.

This must be an original game, not a clone. Do not copy or extract Project Zomboid source code, game files, assets, sprites, animations, audio, music, writing, item descriptions, exact numerical balance, exact recipes, exact professions or traits, exact UI, logos, branding, named locations, building layouts, roads, map data, lore, characters, promotional materials, mods, fan maps, screenshots, or proprietary content. Do not reproduce its map through tracing, screenshots, extracted tile data, fan resources, or one-to-one reconstruction. All code, assets, locations, interfaces, names, world layouts, balancing, writing, visual identity, and implementation details must be original.

CORE PROJECT BRIEF

Create a persistent, multiplayer-first, 2D zombie-survival sandbox designed for:
- Apple iPad 10th Generation
- Safari and home-screen PWA mode
- Landscape orientation
- Touch controls without requiring a keyboard or mouse
- Groups of up to 10 concurrent players
- Private persistent servers
- Desktop browsers as a secondary target

Players must be able to explore a large original region, search buildings, manage supplies, fight or evade infected, treat detailed injuries, manage hunger/thirst/fatigue/temperature/mental condition, establish shelters, barricade and build, farm, cook, craft, repair equipment, operate generators and water systems, maintain vehicles, cooperate or compete, improve practical skills, survive for long periods, die permanently, and continue in the same persistent world with a new character.

The world must continue without the player. Weather, decay, resource depletion, infected migration, power and water failure, vegetation growth, spoilage, fires, vehicles, bodies, and player-built structures persist and progress.

DESIGN PILLARS

1. Vulnerability: The player is an ordinary survivor, not an action hero. Careless decisions, untreated wounds, noise, crashes, fire, infection, or exhaustion can end a long-running character.
2. Systemic depth: Mechanics interact. Rain fills containers; wet clothes lower temperature; illness reduces endurance; wounds affect handling; generators provide electricity while consuming fuel, creating noise, and producing dangerous fumes; food spoils after electricity fails.
3. Emergent storytelling: Stories arise from scarcity, injuries, weather, noise, rescues, failed plans, vehicle breakdowns, fire, cooperation, betrayal, discoveries, and death.
4. Persistent consequences: Broken windows, opened doors, blood, damage, containers, vehicles, bodies, fires, and construction persist. Loot does not magically return by default.
5. Touch-first interaction: Never port a keyboard UI by drawing virtual keyboard buttons over it.
6. Original identity: Create a distinct setting, art direction, interface language, map, outbreak, factions, names, and visual design.

PRACTICAL INITIAL SCOPE

Build a polished vertical slice before expanding:
- One original town and surrounding rural territory
- Residential, commercial, industrial, emergency, forest, and farmland zones

- 3-5 ranged weapon categories
- Four vehicle categories
- Ten-player multiplayer
- Persistent world saving
- Core survival simulation
- Barricading and basic construction
- Touch controls
- A limited but polished crafting system

Do not begin with a continent, 100-player servers, advanced NPC civilization, or thousands of unfinished items.

ORIGINAL WORLD

Design a fictional region with an original name, history, climate, population, industries, architecture, infrastructure, emergency response, outbreak timeline, landmarks, and environmental story. Include a dense town center, neighborhoods, apartments, convenience stores, supermarket, pharmacy, clinic, emergency-services building, school, restaurants, hardware store, fuel station, warehouses, factory, storage units, motel, workshop, farms, barns, forest trails, river or reservoir, utilities, rural homes, checkpoints, camps, service routes, hidden locations, chokepoints, and multiple travel routes.

Buildings need plausible entrances, exits, storage, restrooms, kitchens, utility rooms, staff areas, furniture, windows, loading areas, maintenance access, and loot based on their real function. Use reusable modules without repetitive streets. Use an entirely original coordinate system and tile data.

WORLD STREAMING

Divide the map into streamable chunks. Load only nearby terrain, structures, interactive objects, lighting, audio emitters, infected, vehicles, containers, construction, and effects.

Use three simulation levels:
- Active: full collision, movement, AI, combat, animation, sound response, fire, and interaction near players.
- Reduced: simplified migration, vehicle persistence, generator consumption, plants, spoilage, fire summaries, and environmental progression in nearby unloaded areas.
- Dormant: timestamp-based growth, decay, aggregate infected populations, utility schedules, and structural state far from players.

Prevent duplication, entity loss, teleportation, or corruption when transitioning between simulation levels.

CAMERA AND VISUALS

Support smooth follow, pinch zoom, two-finger pan, indoor visibility, roof/floor hiding, occlusion outlines, adjustable zoom, reduced motion, and configurable camera shake.

Provide Battery Saver, Balanced, and High presets controlling render scale, lighting, shadows, rain, fog, smoke, fire, particles, reflections, vegetation, animation distance, blood persistence, and visible infected detail.

IPAD TOUCH CONTROLS

- Left: adjustable floating or fixed joystick, walk/run sensitivity, sneak toggle, optional auto-walk.
- Right: context action, aim/facing, attack, defensive push, quick equipment, radial menu, and camera gesture zone.
- Gestures: tap, safe double-tap shortcuts, press-and-hold choices, drag-and-drop, pinch zoom, two-finger pan, inventory swipes, and hold-to-transfer.

The context-action system may offer open, close, climb, search, pick up, drink, fill, enter vehicle, treat, move furniture, barricade, repair, start engine, or extinguish fire. It must show action, duration, and clear hazards; support cancellation; honor line-of-sight/elevation; never interact through walls; and prevent accidental consumption or destruction.

Use large targets, safe-area insets, left-handed mode, repositionable/resizable controls, opacity settings, no hover dependency, and no pixel-perfect taps.
